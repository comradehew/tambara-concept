IVOn the day

What the forest asks of you.

A ranger briefing at first light, then a walk that is steep and often wet. A porter from the community carries your bag; we always book one. Trekkers must be fifteen or older, and anyone with a cold stays back to protect the family.

  • Boots

    Ones you have already walked in.

  • A rain jacket

    In any season. The forest is wet all year.

  • Garden gloves

    For the vines you will hold on the way up.

  • Your passport

    We book the permit and send you the Wildlife Authority's receipt.

Questions people ask first.

Can I book a gorilla permit myself?

Not as a foreign visitor. The Uganda Wildlife Authority sells foreign non-resident permits through licensed Ugandan operators. We book yours with your passport details and send you the receipt.

How far ahead should we plan?

Six to twelve months for June to September and December to February. Each family takes eight visitors a day, and the smallest lodges fill first.

How fit do I need to be?

Fit enough to walk several hours on steep ground. Tell us honestly and we will ask for a family that usually stays nearer the trailhead; the rangers make the final call on the day.

Can the journey be fully private?

The guide, vehicle, flights and dining are yours alone. The gorilla hour itself is shared with up to eight visitors, by Wildlife Authority rule, and we will not pretend otherwise.
